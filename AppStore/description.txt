Uniloop — offline 3D single-wheel momentum racing.

Ride an original one-wheel racer through Canyon Ribbon. Balance speed and tilt over banked turns, jumps, and checkpoint gates. Land cleanly, avoid resets, and improve your time and medal. Adaptive menus and controls support keyboard/gamepad on desktop and touch on phones and tablets. Settings include text scale, contrast, reduced shake, audio level, and keyboard remapping; progress saves locally.

Windows Desktop is the current primary build. Android and iOS (phone and tablet) exports remain in development. More courses, ghost replays, and JSON record exchange are planned, not shipped. No account, ads, or trackers.

Pre-release copy; verify all features on each platform before store publication.
