Uniloop is an offline, single-player 3D momentum racer where one wheel is all you need.

Ride a unicycle along twisting striped rails, launch into loops, choose alternate routes, and turn clean landings into points and speed boosts. Keep your momentum, jump over obstacles and goo, and push for a faster finish. Wipeouts cost points and bring your rider to a stop, so every landing matters.

SIX ORIGINAL TRACKS
Warm up on Sunny Sprint, tackle the muddy rails of Canyon Ribbon, race the glowing loops of Neon Night, take on Goo Gauntlet, and smash through The Monopoly. Earn bronze medals on any two courses to unlock the skyline gaps and short landings of Rooftop Precision. The original five courses remain open.

TRICKS, SPEED, AND MEDALS
Pull off flips, rolls, side-flips, and tabletops in the air. Land cleanly to build your score and earn a burst of speed. Finish each race to see your time, medal, points, stunt count, top speed, and wipeouts, then chase your local best records in the League screen. Your best captured run races beside you as a ghost replaying its saved controls, including falls and checkpoint resets.

YOUR WHEEL, YOUR CONTROLS
Choose from six unicycle colours and play with a keyboard or gamepad. Remap keyboard controls and adjust text size, high contrast, reduced motion, camera shake, and separate music and sound-effects volume.

OFFLINE BY DESIGN
No account, ads, analytics, or internet connection required. Your settings and records stay saved locally on your device. Uniloop is a solo racing game, with no online multiplayer or live leaderboards.

One wheel. Six tracks. Keep the momentum.
